# Human physical-reference annotation provenance

Haoming Wang manually annotated the stationary-camera support poles in these 12 CVAT XML files. Together they cover 61 unique retained images and contain 199 traces: 172 marked usable and 27 marked unusable through the CVAT `status` attribute.

Each trace runs from ground contact to the nominal camera mounting or optical-center height of a 5-ft (152.4-cm) support pole. Labels `pole1` through `pole4` distinguish visible poles within an image; the numeric suffix does not encode distance or pole length.

The confirmed duplicate `C-039_2021-07-30JPG.JPG` is omitted. `C-039_2021-07-30.JPG` is the retained image.

These 2021 support poles differ from the 2024–2025 red-band calibration poles. The four 35-in intervals in the installation records describe horizontal field layout and are not marks on the support poles.
