# Editorial fit and acceptance estimate

## Current estimate after this revision

Estimated eventual acceptance probability at *The Plant Phenome Journal*: **65–75%**, with a working midpoint of **70%**.

This is a judgment estimate, not a journal statistic. The journal currently reports an overall acceptance rate of 64%. The manuscript is above that baseline on scope fit, methods transparency, independent manual-reference validation, and public reproducibility. Its main downward pressure comes from the pilot biological sample, uncertain gains over the two strongest comparators, broad uncertainty intervals, incomplete raw-image provenance for 69 validation records, and the absence of a formal cost study.

Before the journal-specific revision, the estimated range was **55–65%**. The gain comes from choosing the Methods and Techniques article type, satisfying the journal's front-matter and formatting requirements, tightening the central novelty claim, separating claim-specific evidence, adding crop-phenomics and affordable-phenomics context, clarifying transfer requirements, and providing a submission-ready reproducibility package.

## Highest-value remaining actions without new data

1. Screen preferred reviewers carefully for conflicts and choose at least two with complementary image-analysis and field-phenomics expertise.
2. Use the supplied cover letter and significance statement so the handling editor sees the Methods and Techniques fit immediately.
3. Complete author ORCID fields in ScholarOne where available.
4. During proofing, preserve the bounded comparative language; claiming a decisive advantage over the single-frame or Gaussian comparator would reduce credibility.

No defensible manuscript-only revision can remove the remaining data limitations. The present package maximizes acceptance prospects by making the contribution useful, reproducible, and precisely supported rather than by enlarging claims beyond the evidence.
