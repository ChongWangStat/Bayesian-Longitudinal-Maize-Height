TPPJ supplies a journal-specific Word template and accepts LaTeX manuscripts, but it does not supply a journal-specific LaTeX class. The authoritative `manuscript/main.tex` therefore uses standard LaTeX while reproducing the current TPPJ template requirements:

- US-letter paper with 1-inch margins
- 12-point Times-family type
- double-spaced manuscript with continuous line numbers
- title, author byline, complete postal affiliations, corresponding-author details, and alphabetical abbreviations on the first page
- Plain Language Summary followed by the Abstract on the second manuscript page
- numbered Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, and Discussion sections
- Conclusions as the final subsection of Discussion and below 200 words
- figures and tables embedded near their first citations and supplied separately as PDFs
- APA author–year citations and an alphabetized reference list
- separate Acknowledgments, Author Contributions, Funding, Conflict of Interest, ORCID, Supplemental Material, Data Availability Statement, and AI declaration headings

The corresponding-author ORCID is `0000-0002-2093-8018`. All required manuscript fields are populated. The other authors' ORCIDs are optional and can be entered in ScholarOne if they choose to provide them.

For a LaTeX submission, ScholarOne still requires an intentionally blank Word file to be designated as the main document. The complete review manuscript is the PDF, and the editable source is the LaTeX ZIP. This upload arrangement follows the journal's official LaTeX instructions.
